# 2-minute demo script

0:00–0:15  
"Students do not get stuck because they need the final answer. They get stuck on the next move. DoubtLens reads the worksheet photo and teaches that move."

0:15–0:35  
Open the app. Click Algebra worksheet. Click Read the page.  
"It saw the equation. Hint first: expand the brackets."

0:35–1:00  
Switch to Next step.  
"It does not say x = 11. It says rewrite 3(x - 2) as 3x - 6."

1:00–1:25  
Type `3x - 2` in I tried this. Click Check my step.  
"Off-track. It catches the common bracket mistake."

1:25–1:45  
Clear and type `3x - 6`. Check again.  
"On-track. Now it tells the student to combine -6 and +5."

1:45–2:00  
"Full solution stays locked until they ask. That is the product: multimodal tutoring, not answer dumping."
